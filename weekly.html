Effect of Screening with Multicancer Early-Detection Test on Late-Stage Cancer Diagnosis

In the NHS-Galleri randomized trial in England, 142,250 adults aged 50-77 were assigned to annual blood-based multicancer early-detection testing plus usual care or usual care alone for three rounds. The incidence of stage III-IV cancer across 12 prespecified cancers did not differ (incidence rate ratio 1.03; 95% CI 0.92-1.14; P=0.63). Stage IV cancer rate ratio was 0.86 (95% CI 0.74-1.00). Fewer than 1% had trial-related adverse events.

英國 NHS-Galleri 隨機試驗納入 142,250 名 50–77 歲成人，比較三輪年度多癌症早期偵測血液檢測加常規照護與常規照護。12 種預設癌症之 III–IV 期發生率無顯著差異（發生率比 1.03，95% CI 0.92–1.14，P=0.63）；IV 期發生率比 0.86（0.74–1.00）。試驗相關不良事件低於 1%。

NEJM 心血管

Anticoagulation for Atrial Fibrillation with Intermediate Stroke Risk

SINGLE-AF, a multicenter open-label trial in South Korea, randomized 1803 patients with atrial fibrillation and intermediate stroke risk (CHA2DS2-VASc 1 in men, 2 in women) to DOAC therapy or no anticoagulation. At 24 months the composite of stroke, systemic embolism, major bleeding, or cardiovascular death occurred in 0.5% versus 1.5% (HR 0.31; 95% CI 0.10-0.94; P=0.03). Stroke occurred in 0.3% versus 1.1%; major bleeding was similar.

南韓多中心開放性 SINGLE-AF 試驗將 1803 名中度中風風險（男 CHA2DS2-VASc 1 分、女 2 分）心房顫動患者隨機分派接受 DOAC 或不抗凝。24 個月時中風、全身性栓塞、重大出血或心血管死亡複合終點為 0.5% 對 1.5%（HR 0.31，95% CI 0.10–0.94，P=0.03）；中風 0.3% 對 1.1%，重大出血相近。

Oveporexton for Narcolepsy Type 1 - Results from Two Phase 3 Trials

Two phase 3 randomized placebo-controlled trials (First Light, n=168; Radiant Light, n=105) evaluated oveporexton, an oral orexin receptor 2 agonist, over 12 weeks in narcolepsy type 1. Mean sleep latency on the Maintenance of Wakefulness Test improved 14.3-19.8 minutes versus -0.4 to -0.8 with placebo; Epworth scores fell 9.7-11.8 points versus 1.5-1.7; weekly cataplexy decreased 79-89% versus 28-39% (all P<0.001). Urinary frequency and transient insomnia were common.

兩項第三期隨機安慰劑對照試驗（First Light 168 人、Radiant Light 105 人）評估口服 orexin 2 受體促效劑 oveporexton 治療第一型猝睡症 12 週。清醒維持測試睡眠潛時改善 14.3–19.8 分鐘（安慰劑 -0.4 至 -0.8）；Epworth 嗜睡量表下降 9.7–11.8 分（安慰劑 1.5–1.7）；每週猝倒減少 79–89%（安慰劑 28–39%），皆 P<0.001。常見頻尿與暫時性失眠。

Faecal microbiota transplantation in irritable bowel syndrome (REFIT2): a randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled, phase 3 trial

REFIT2, a phase 3 double-blind trial at five Norwegian hospitals, randomized 450 adults with moderate-to-severe IBS (IBS-SSS >=175) 2:1 to a single rectal enema of donor faeces or autologous placebo. At 90 days, 40% of the donor group and 38% of the placebo group achieved a >=75-point IBS-SSS reduction (difference 1.9 percentage points; 95% CI -8.1 to 12.0; P=0.76). Adverse events were similar, showing no clinical benefit of FMT in IBS.

挪威五家醫院之第三期雙盲 REFIT2 試驗，將 450 名中重度大腸激躁症患者（IBS-SSS ≥175）以 2:1 隨機分派接受單次捐贈者糞便或自體安慰劑直腸灌注。90 天時 IBS-SSS 下降 ≥75 分者分別為 40% 與 38%（差異 1.9 個百分點，95% CI -8.1 至 12.0，P=0.76），不良事件相近，未顯示糞菌移植對 IBS 有臨床效益。

Switch to injectable cabotegravir-rilpivirine given every 8 weeks in adolescents living with HIV with virological suppression in sub-Saharan Africa (LATA): a randomised, open-label, multicentre, 96-week non-inferiority trial

LATA randomized 476 virologically suppressed adolescents (12 to <20 years) with HIV in Kenya, South Africa, Uganda and Zimbabwe to long-acting injectable cabotegravir-rilpivirine every 8 weeks or daily oral dolutegravir-lamivudine-tenofovir. By week 96, confirmed viral load >=50 copies/mL occurred in 0.9% of the injectable group versus 6.4% of controls (difference -5.5%; 99% CI -10.3 to -1.5), meeting non-inferiority and demonstrating superiority (P=0.0013). Serious adverse events were similar.

LATA 試驗在肯亞、南非、烏干達及辛巴威將 476 名病毒抑制之 HIV 青少年（12 至未滿 20 歲）隨機分派接受每 8 週長效注射 cabotegravir–rilpivirine 或每日口服 dolutegravir–lamivudine–tenofovir。至 96 週，確認病毒量 ≥50 copies/mL 者分別為 0.9% 與 6.4%（差異 -5.5%，99% CI -10.3 至 -1.5），達非劣性並證明優越性（P=0.0013）；嚴重不良事件相近。

Addition of High-Dose Vitamin D3 to Standard Treatment in Patients With Metastatic Colorectal Cancer: The SOLARIS Randomized Clinical Trial (Alliance A021703)

Double-blind phase 3 trial of 455 previously untreated metastatic colorectal cancer patients receiving mFOLFOX6 or FOLFIRI plus bevacizumab, randomized to high-dose vitamin D3 (8000 IU loading then 4000 IU daily) or standard 400 IU daily. Median progression-free survival was 11.8 vs 10.3 months (1-sided P = .25); objective response 51% vs 44% (P = .12); median overall survival 25.6 vs 27.0 months. High-dose vitamin D3 did not improve PFS, contradicting earlier phase 2 findings.

第三期雙盲試驗納入455位未曾治療的轉移性大腸直腸癌患者，在化療加bevacizumab基礎上隨機給予高劑量（負荷8000 IU後每日4000 IU）或標準400 IU維生素D3。中位無惡化存活11.8對10.3個月（P=.25），客觀反應率51%對44%，總存活25.6對27.0個月，高劑量維生素D3未改善預後。

JAMA 腎臟／代謝

Dapagliflozin and Acute Kidney Injury Following Cardiac Surgery: A Randomized Clinical Trial

Multicenter double-blind placebo-controlled trial in the Netherlands randomized 784 adults undergoing elective cardiac surgery to dapagliflozin 10 mg or placebo daily from the day before surgery through postoperative day 2 (4 doses). Acute kidney injury by KDIGO criteria within 7 days occurred in 28% vs 52% (relative risk 0.54; 95% CI 0.45-0.65; P < .001). Atrial fibrillation (45% both groups) and reoperation (11% vs 10%) were similar. Brief perioperative SGLT2 inhibition reduced postoperative AKI.

荷蘭多中心雙盲試驗將784位接受擇期心臟手術的成人隨機分至術前一天起每日dapagliflozin 10 mg或安慰劑共4劑。術後7天內KDIGO定義急性腎損傷發生率28%對52%（相對風險0.54，95% CI 0.45-0.65，P<.001），心房顫動（各45%）與再手術率相似，短期周術期SGLT2抑制劑可降低急性腎損傷。

Stereotactic Body Radiotherapy vs Moderately Hypofractionated IMRT for Localized Intermediate-Risk Prostate Cancer: A Randomized Clinical Trial

NRG-GU005 phase 3 trial at 136 centers randomized 698 men with intermediate-risk localized prostate cancer to SBRT (36.25 Gy in 5 fractions) or moderately hypofractionated IMRT (70 Gy/28 or 60 Gy/20 fractions). At 2 years, urinary irritative decline was similar (35.4% vs 33.7%), but SBRT had fewer bowel-domain declines (34.9% vs 43.8%; P = .03) and fewer grade 3-4 genitourinary events (0.6% vs 2.5%). Three-year disease-free survival was 88.6% for SBRT vs 92.1% for IMRT; SBRT was not superior.

NRG-GU005第三期試驗於136個中心將698位中風險局限性攝護腺癌患者隨機分至SBRT（36.25 Gy分5次）或中度低分次IMRT。2年時泌尿刺激症狀惡化比例相似（35.4%對33.7%），但SBRT腸道生活品質惡化較少（34.9%對43.8%，P=.03）、3-4級泌尿不良事件較少（0.6%對2.5%）；3年無病存活88.6%對92.1%，SBRT未顯優越。

COVID-19 Vaccine Effectiveness and Safety for the 2026-2027 Respiratory Season

Systematic review of studies published August 2025 to June 2026 on US-licensed COVID-19 vaccines identified 155 eligible publications (15 RCTs, 84 comparative observational studies). Updated vaccines were associated with reduced hospitalization in adults 65 or older (vaccine effectiveness 53%; 95% CI 37%-65%) and adults 18-64 (54%). Maternal vaccination reduced infant COVID-19 hospital contacts (VE 50%-54%); pediatric ED visits fell 76%. Safety profiles were consistent with prior reviews, with no new signals including myocarditis.

系統性回顧2025年8月至2026年6月發表的155篇研究（含15項隨機試驗、84項有對照觀察性研究）。更新版COVID-19疫苗使65歲以上成人住院風險降低53%（95% CI 37%-65%），18-64歲成人降低54%；孕期接種使嬰兒出生後2個月內COVID相關住院減少50%-54%，兒童急診就診減少76%。未發現新的安全性疑慮，包括心肌炎。

Impact of Family History on Colorectal Cancer Risk in Inflammatory Bowel Disease and in Matched General Population Comparators

Swedish nationwide register cohort (1996-2023) of 124,387 IBD patients and 1,213,641 matched comparators, median 11 years follow-up. Colorectal cancer incidence was 1.17 vs 0.88 per 1000 person-years (1882 vs 14,177 events). In IBD, having two or more affected first-degree relatives added 2.69 cases per 1000 person-years, whereas early-onset CRC heredity added only 0.42. Absolute effects of family history were similar in IBD and comparators, suggesting surveillance should prioritize multiple affected relatives.

瑞典全國登錄世代（1996-2023）納入124,387名IBD患者與1,213,641名配對對照，中位追蹤11年。大腸癌發生率分別為每1000人年1.17與0.88（1882對14,177例）。IBD患者若有兩位以上一級親屬罹癌，每1000人年多出2.69例；早發性大腸癌家族史僅多0.42例。家族史的絕對效應在IBD與對照相似，提示監測策略應優先針對多位親屬罹癌者，而非僅早發家族史。

Individualized Targeted Exclusion Diet Based on Confocal Laser Endomicroscopy Does Not Improve Irritable Bowel Syndrome Symptoms: A Randomized Controlled Crossover Trial

Double-blind randomized crossover trial (NCT05097872) testing whether confocal laser endomicroscopy can identify culprit foods in irritable bowel syndrome. Patients excluded up to two CLE-reactive foods (real diet) or two non-reactive foods (sham diet) for 4 weeks each. Response rates did not differ (42% vs 36%; odds ratio 1.33; P=.6), and symptom scores fell similarly (-30 vs -20; P=.7). Mucosal alterations appeared in 100% of 15 healthy volunteers, indicating nonspecific reactions.

此雙盲隨機交叉試驗（NCT05097872）檢驗共軛焦雷射內視鏡顯微術（CLE）能否找出大腸激躁症的致病食物。患者分別排除兩種CLE有反應（真飲食）與兩種無反應食物（假飲食）各4週。反應率無差異（42%對36%；勝算比1.33；P=.6），症狀分數改善相近（-30對-20；P=.7）。15名健康志願者黏膜變化達100%，顯示屬非特異性反應，不支持CLE臨床應用。

Dietary modulation of intestinal microbes prolongs survival in a Hirschsprung disease mouse model

In the piebald lethal (sl/sl) Hirschsprung disease mouse model, diet dramatically altered survival. A detrimental diet raised colonic epithelial oxygen, damaged mitochondria and expanded Enterobacteriaceae. A protective diet promoted epithelial hypoxia, raised antimicrobial peptides and fecal butyrate, improved barrier function, reduced Enterobacteriaceae and tripled median lifespan. Tungstate inhibition of Enterobacteriaceae anaerobic respiration also improved survival, suggesting dietary strategies might prevent Hirschsprung-associated enterocolitis.

在赫氏腸症小鼠模型（piebald lethal, sl/sl）中，飲食顯著影響存活。不良飲食升高大腸上皮氧濃度、損傷粒線體並使腸內菌科增生；保護性飲食促進上皮缺氧、提升抗菌胜肽與糞便丁酸、改善屏障功能、減少腸內菌科，並使中位壽命延長為三倍。以鎢酸鹽抑制腸內菌科厭氧呼吸亦改善存活，提示飲食策略或可預防赫氏腸症相關腸炎。

Bioengineering a Probiotic to Bloom During Colonic Inflammation Promotes Reliable Efficacy in Translational Models of Colitis

Escherichia coli Nissle 1917 was engineered with the ttr operon (EcN::ttr) so it can use tetrathionate, an inflammation byproduct, as fuel. In acute dextran sodium sulfate and chronic mucin 2-deficient colitis models, EcN::ttr outperformed parent EcN and 5-aminosalicylate, blooming during inflammation and driving interleukin 10-dependent immunoregulation with less leukocyte infiltration, higher butyrate, reduced bacterial translocation and better zonula occludens-1 organization in human colonoids.

研究者將ttr操縱子導入大腸桿菌Nissle 1917（EcN::ttr），使其能利用發炎產物四硫磺酸鹽為能源。在急性DSS與慢性mucin 2缺陷結腸炎模型中，EcN::ttr療效優於原始EcN與5-胺基柳酸，能於發炎期增生，透過依賴介白素10的免疫調節減少白血球浸潤、提高丁酸、降低細菌移生，並改善人類腸道類器官的zonula occludens-1排列，為IBD菌相療法概念驗證。

Clinical utility of a 30% reduction in VCTE-derived liver stiffness measurement for identifying histologic improvement in MASH

Prospective analysis of 160 adults with biopsy-proven MASH and F2-3 fibrosis from a phase 2b pegozafermin trial, with paired VCTE and biopsy. A >=30% relative decline in liver stiffness had an AUC of 0.68 (95% CI 0.58-0.77) for fibrosis regression without MASH worsening (adjusted OR 4.23), and AUC 0.62 in a 48-patient validation cohort. Accuracy was modest; better treatment-response biomarkers are needed.

納入 pegozafermin 第 2b 期試驗中 160 位切片證實 MASH 且 F2-3 纖維化的成人，同步比對 VCTE 與切片。肝硬度值相對下降 ≥30% 預測纖維化消退且 MASH 未惡化的 AUC 為 0.68（校正 OR 4.23），48 人驗證組 AUC 為 0.62。準確度僅屬中等，仍需更佳的治療反應生物標記。

Beyond cirrhosis: Major adverse liver outcomes across homozygous and heterozygous alpha-1 antitrypsin deficiency-associated liver disease

Retrospective US Veterans cohort (2000-2025) of 22,537 genotyped participants (19,665 Pi*MM, 1,656 Pi*MZ, 281 Pi*SZ, 935 Pi*ZZ) with 352,612 person-years.
