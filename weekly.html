NEJM 心血管介入

Complete Revascularization Guided by Functional Coronary Angiography in STEMI

In 1823 patients with STEMI and multivessel disease, complete revascularization guided by functional coronary angiography was compared with conventional angiography guidance. At a median 17.9 months, primary events (death, MI, cerebrovascular accident, or ischemia-driven revascularization) occurred in 8.9% versus 13.7% (HR 0.62). Contrast-associated acute kidney injury or major bleeding also favored the physiology-guided group (4.6% vs 7.1%, HR 0.63).

1823名ST段上升心肌梗塞合併多血管病變病人隨機分為生理學指引或傳統攝影指引完全血管重建。中位追蹤17.9個月，主要終點（死亡、心肌梗塞、腦血管事件或缺血導致再介入）發生率8.9%對13.7%（HR 0.62）。顯影劑腎損傷或大出血也較低（4.6%對7.1%，HR 0.63），支持以功能性評估挑選非罪犯病灶。

NEJM 呼吸道疾病

Tozorakimab to Prevent COPD Exacerbations

Two replicate phase 3 trials (OBERON, TITANIA) randomized adults with exacerbating COPD to subcutaneous tozorakimab, an anti-interleukin-33 antibody, 300 mg every 4 weeks or placebo for 52 weeks, without eosinophil entry criteria. Annualized moderate or severe exacerbations fell in the overall population (rate ratios 0.70 and 0.71) and among former smokers (0.71 and 0.66). Adverse event rates were comparable with placebo.

兩項平行第三期試驗（OBERON、TITANIA）將有急性發作史的COPD成人隨機分配皮下tozorakimab（抗IL-33單株抗體）300毫克每4週一次或安慰劑，共52週，且未設嗜異性白血球門檻。整體族群中重度急性發作年化率降低（rate ratio 0.70與0.71），戒菸族群亦降低（0.71與0.66），不良事件發生率與安慰劑相當。

NEJM 血液腫瘤

Treatment Decisions in Multiple Myeloma

This review offers a practical framework for multiple myeloma, now moving toward functional cure. Cytogenetic and genomic risk, functional fitness, and measurable residual disease individualize newly diagnosed therapy, where quadruplet induction is standard and the role of autologous transplantation is being reassessed. At relapse, sequencing of BCMA-directed CAR T cells, bispecific antibodies, and antibody-drug conjugates should weigh T-cell fitness and antigen escape.

本篇綜論提出多發性骨髓瘤的實用決策架構，該病已朝「功能性治癒」邁進。初診病人應以細胞遺傳學與基因體風險、功能體能狀態及可測量殘存疾病個人化治療，四藥induction已成標準，自體移植角色正重新評估。復發時CAR-T、雙特異性抗體與抗體藥物複合體的排序須考量T細胞活力與抗原逃脫。

Lancet 胃癌治療

Perioperative durvalumab plus FLOT for resectable gastric and gastro-oesophageal junction adenocarcinoma (MATTERHORN): final overall survival results

MATTERHORN randomized 948 adults with resectable stage II-IVa gastric or gastro-oesophageal junction adenocarcinoma across 147 centres in 20 countries to perioperative durvalumab plus FLOT chemotherapy or placebo plus FLOT. Overall survival improved significantly with durvalumab (hazard ratio 0.78, 95% CI 0.63-0.96; p=0.021). Treatment-related deaths occurred in 1% versus under 1%, establishing this regimen as a new standard option.

MATTERHORN在20國147中心納入948名可手術第II至IVa期胃癌或胃食道接合處腺癌病人，隨機分為術前後durvalumab併FLOT化療或安慰劑併FLOT。durvalumab組總存活期顯著改善（HR 0.78，95% CI 0.63-0.96，p=0.021），治療相關死亡為1%對不到1%。此組合成為可切除胃癌的新標準治療選項之一。

Induction and consolidation atezolizumab with stereotactic body radiation therapy versus radiation alone in high-risk, early-stage non-small-cell lung cancer (SWOG/NRG S1914)

In 402 evaluable patients with medically inoperable T1-T3N0M0 non-small-cell lung cancer and recurrence risk factors, adding neoadjuvant, concurrent, and adjuvant atezolizumab to stereotactic body radiation therapy did not improve overall survival (hazard ratio 1.04). Two-year overall survival was 82% in both groups, while grade 3 or higher adverse events rose from 3% to 12%. Accrual closed early for futility.

Loberamisal for Acute Ischemic Stroke: The LAIS Randomized Clinical Trial

This phase 3 trial at 32 Chinese hospitals randomized 997 adults with acute ischemic stroke (NIHSS 7-20) presenting within 48 hours to intravenous loberamisal 40 mg daily for 10 days or placebo. At 90 days, 69.7% versus 56.3% achieved a modified Rankin Scale score of 0 to 1 (relative risk 1.24; risk difference 13.3 percentage points). Adverse events were similar between groups.

中國32家醫院第三期試驗納入997名發病48小時內、NIHSS 7至20分的急性缺血性腦中風病人，隨機給予靜脈loberamisal 40毫克每日一次共10天或安慰劑。90天時達完全功能恢復（mRS 0-1分）者為69.7%對56.3%（相對風險1.24，絕對差13.3個百分點），兩組不良事件相當，仍待其他族群驗證。

Osimertinib With or Without Chemotherapy in Advanced Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer With EGFR and Concurrent TP53 Mutations

This multicentre phase 3 trial randomized 294 treatment-naive patients with stage IV non-squamous non-small cell lung cancer harbouring both EGFR-sensitizing and TP53 mutations to osimertinib plus pemetrexed-carboplatin or osimertinib alone. Median progression-free survival was 34.0 versus 15.6 months (hazard ratio 0.44; p<0.001), consistent across subgroups including brain metastases. Grade 3 or higher toxicity was higher with combination therapy.

多中心第三期試驗納入294名未曾治療、第IV期非鱗狀非小細胞肺癌且同時帶EGFR致敏突變與TP53突變病人，隨機分為osimertinib併pemetrexed加carboplatin或單用osimertinib。中位無惡化存活期34.0對15.6個月（HR 0.44，p<0.001），各次族群包含腦轉移者一致；合併治療3級以上毒性較高。

JAMA 重症營養

Gastric Residual Volume Assessment in Critically Ill Children: The GASTRIC-PICU Randomized Clinical Trial

This pragmatic noninferiority trial in 24 paediatric intensive care units randomized 4700 mechanically ventilated children starting enteral feeds to no routine gastric residual volume assessment or six-hourly assessment. Ventilator-free days and survival at 30 days were noninferior (median 25 days in both groups; adjusted odds ratio 0.95). Energy requirements met by 72 hours rose from 76.8% to 80.3%, favouring omission of routine checks.

24家兒科加護病房的實務型非劣效試驗，將4700名接受插管通氣並開始腸道餵食的兒童隨機分為不常規測胃殘餘量或每6小時測量。30天無呼吸器天數與存活為非劣效（兩組中位均25天，調整後OR 0.95）；72小時內達熱量需求比例由76.8%升至80.3%，支持取消常規測量以改善營養輸送。

JAMA 疫苗與感染症

Influenza Vaccine Effectiveness and Safety for the 2026-2027 Respiratory Season

This systematic review of 69 studies published August 2025 to June 2026 found influenza vaccines reduced influenza-related hospitalization (vaccine effectiveness 19% to 43%) and mortality (29% to 66%) in older adults. Effectiveness against laboratory-confirmed influenza-associated death in children aged 6 months to 17 years was 80%. A self-controlled case series of nearly 10 million people found no increase in serious adverse events.

系統回顧納入2025年8月至2026年6月發表的69篇研究，顯示流感疫苗可降低老年人流感相關住院（疫苗效益19%至43%）與死亡（29%至66%）。6個月至17歲兒少對實驗室確診流感相關死亡的疫苗效益達80%。近1000萬人自我對照病例系列未發現嚴重不良事件增加，包含與RSV或COVID疫苗併用。

Interventions for Increasing Colorectal Cancer Screening Uptake: A Systematic Review and Network Meta-Analysis

This network meta-analysis of 76 randomized trials compared eight strategies for increasing colorectal cancer screening uptake. Patient navigation (risk ratio 1.58) and mailed faecal immunochemical test outreach (1.36) significantly outperformed usual care, while choice-based and colonoscopy outreach did not. Mailed FIT was most effective where baseline uptake was below 30% (risk ratio 3.12); educational multimedia performed best in higher-uptake populations.

網絡統合分析納入76項隨機試驗，比較八種提高大腸癌篩檢參與率的策略。病人導航（RR 1.58）與郵寄糞便免疫化學檢查（FIT）外展（RR 1.36）顯著優於常規照護，選擇式與大腸鏡外展則無差異。基礎參與率低於30%者以郵寄FIT最有效（RR 3.12），參與率較高族群則以教育多媒體表現最佳。

Achalasia and Risk of Esophageal Squamous Cell Carcinoma and Adenocarcinoma in a Multinational Study

This population-based case-control study in five Nordic countries matched 35,604 esophageal cancer cases to 355,869 controls from 1987 to 2023. Achalasia was strongly associated with esophageal squamous cell carcinoma (odds ratio 9.46), rising to 16.33 after myotomy or dilation. The association with adenocarcinoma was moderate (3.05) but attenuated to 1.44 after adjustment for gastroesophageal reflux disease.

北歐五國族群性病例對照研究，1987至2023年將35,604名食道癌病例與355,869名對照配對。食道賁門失能症與食道鱗狀細胞癌風險強烈相關（OR 9.46），接受肌肉切開或擴張術者更高達16.33。與腺癌的關聯中等（OR 3.05），但校正胃食道逆流後降至1.44，顯示腺癌風險多由逆流解釋。

Short-Term Success, Long-Term Failure: Strain Turnover and Virulence Re-Emergence May Drive Relapse in Pouchitis

Twenty-one patients with pouchitis after ileal pouch-anal anastomosis received two weeks of metronidazole plus ciprofloxacin or doxycycline, providing 130 stool samples over one year. Faecal calprotectin fell from 728 to 265 micrograms per gram and bacterial exotoxin genes declined, but both rebounded by six weeks. Gastroenterology 腸道與神經退化

Protein Misfolding Enteropathy Predicts and Prognosticates Neurodegenerative Disease Years Before Symptom Onset

Archival gastrointestinal biopsies from 196 individuals with unexplained symptoms and 13 to 15 years of follow-up were stained for misfolded TDP-43, tau, and alpha-synuclein. Protein misfolding enteropathy appeared in 60% and predicted non-Alzheimer dementia or alpha-synucleinopathies with over 80% sensitivity but low specificity. Two or more markers reduced survival dose-dependently, and changes preceded neurologic symptoms by 6.9 years.

Hepatocyte MLKL drives obesity-driven hepatocellular carcinoma progression via mitochondrial dysfunction independent of necroptosis in MASLD

In a long-term western-diet model of MASLD-related hepatocellular carcinoma, hepatocyte-specific MLKL knockout mice developed fewer and smaller tumours with reduced proliferation and stemness, without changes in inflammation or fibrosis. MLKL acted independently of necroptosis by suppressing mitofusin 2 and impairing mitochondrial respiration. The MLKL inhibitor necrosulfonamide suppressed HepG2 xenograft growth, and high MLKL expression in human HCC correlated with poorer survival.

Hepatocyte hnRNPK preserves mitochondrial integrity to restrain liver injury and fibrosis

Reduced hepatocyte hnRNPK was a conserved hallmark of liver fibrosis across MASH, alcohol-related liver disease, hepatitis B, and drug-induced injury in humans and mice. Hepatocyte-specific Hnrnpk deletion caused aberrant Opa1 splicing, L-OPA1 depletion, and severe mitochondrial fragmentation, activating stellate cells via the mtDNA-cGAS-STING pathway. With no lysine kinase 1 in hepatic stellate cells promotes mouse and human liver fibrosis through glycolytic triosephosphate isomerase 1

A phospho-kinase array identified WNK1 as a kinase activated by PDGF-B in primary human hepatic stellate cells. Chemical genetics revealed the glycolytic enzyme triosephosphate isomerase 1 as a direct WNK1 substrate within phase-separated condensates. WNK1 inhibition reduced stellate cell glycolysis, proliferation, and migration, attenuated fibrosis in carbon tetrachloride and diet-induced mouse models, and reduced fibrogenesis in human precision-cut liver slices.

Association between statin use and hepatic decompensation in patients with primary biliary cholangitis: A target trial emulation study

Using electronic health records from Boston and Seoul, this sequential target trial emulation matched 443 statin users with primary biliary cholangitis to 886 non-users. Over a median 3.8 years, hepatic decompensation occurred in 5.4% versus 7.6% (hazard ratio 0.61), and major adverse liver outcomes were also reduced (hazard ratio 0.58). Results were consistent across both data sources and cirrhosis status.

In the per-protocol population of 275 participants aged 8 to 17 with stage 3 type 1 diabetes, two 12-day courses of teplizumab preserved beta cell function versus placebo, with a least squares mean difference in stimulated C-peptide of 0.14 nmol/L at week 78. Human versus analogue insulin for children and young adults with type 1 diabetes in low-resource settings (HumAn-1): a multicentre, open-label, randomised controlled trial

HumAn-1 randomized 400 participants aged 7 to 25 with type 1 diabetes in Bangladesh and Tanzania to insulin glargine or continued isophane-based usual care. At six months, blinded continuous glucose monitoring showed no difference in time below 3 mmol/L (adjusted mean difference 0.22%) or time in target range (0.55%). Evaluating Improvement in Pain and Sensory Function When Using High-Frequency (10-kHz) Spinal Cord Stimulation in Individuals With Painful Diabetic Neuropathy: A Multicenter Randomized Controlled Trial (PDN-Sensory)

PDN-Sensory randomized 91 participants with painful diabetic neuropathy to conventional medical management alone or with 10-kHz spinal cord stimulation. In a worst-case analysis, 27 of 34 stimulation participants achieved at least 50% pain relief versus 2 of 50 controls. Executive Summary of the ASN Kidney Health Guidance on the Cardiovascular-Kidney-Metabolic Syndrome

This ASN guidance positions kidney disease at the centre of cardiovascular-kidney-metabolic syndrome. It recommends integrating the PREVENT equation with kidney outcome predictors for routine risk assessment, with lifestyle modification underpinning care. Renin-angiotensin system inhibitors and SGLT2 inhibitors are foundational, while GLP-1-based therapies and non-steroidal mineralocorticoid receptor antagonists may be added according to individual risk, avoiding therapeutic inertia.

美國腎臟醫學會此份指引將腎臟疾病置於心腎代謝（CKM）症候群核心，建議routine風險評估整合PREVENT公式與腎臟結果預測工具，並以生活型態調整為基礎。RAS抑制劑與SGLT2抑制劑為基礎用藥，可依個人心血管與代謝風險加上GLP-1類藥物及非類固醇型礦物皮質素受體拮抗劑，並強調避免治療惰性。

Associations of Plant- and Animal-Sourced Ultra-Processed Foods and Incident CKD in the Cardiovascular Health Study

Among 2661 adults aged 65 and older followed a median 6.8 years, the highest tertiles of total and animal-sourced ultra-processed food intake carried 26% and 37% higher risk of incident chronic kidney disease. Plant-sourced ultra-processed food showed a modest association per standard deviation. Higher minimally processed food intake was associated with 20% lower risk, with no associations for eGFR change.

2661名65歲以上成人中位追蹤6.8年，總超加工食品與動物來源超加工食品攝取最高三分位者，新發慢性腎臟病風險分別高出26%與37%。植物來源超加工食品每增加一個標準差風險略升（HR 1.07）。最低加工食品攝取較高者風險低20%，但與eGFR變化無關，提示植物食品的好處可能被過度加工削弱。

Randomized Controlled Trial Comparing Lactoferrin Plus Iron to Iron Alone for Iron Deficiency Anemia in CKD

This open-label trial randomized 185 patients with chronic kidney disease and iron deficiency anaemia, 90 not on dialysis and 95 dialysis-dependent, to oral liposomal iron with or without lactoferrin 100 mg twice daily for three months. Haemoglobin rose by 1.95 versus 1.33 g/dL in non-dialysis patients and 1.73 versus 1.11 g/dL in dialysis patients, with greater gains in iron indices.

開放標籤試驗納入185名慢性腎臟病合併缺鐵性貧血病人（90名未洗腎、95名洗腎依賴），隨機給予口服微脂體鐵劑加或不加乳鐵蛋白100毫克每日兩次共3個月。未洗腎組血色素上升1.95對1.33 g/dL，洗腎組1.73對1.11 g/dL，鐵相關指標改善亦較佳，發炎指標有輕度改善但不一致。
